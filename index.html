Robotics for a
changing world.

RISTRA is an interdisciplinary center at The University of New Mexico advancing robotic intelligent systems with trustworthy autonomy. We connect researchers, students, and partners to help autonomous technologies operate capably in complex, changing environments.

Our work spans robotics, artificial intelligence, control, and human-centered research. We study how autonomous systems perceive their surroundings, make decisions under uncertainty, and work alongside people—and how to make those systems safe, resilient, transparent, and worthy of trust.

Grounded in Albuquerque and connected to research challenges around the world, RISTRA brings foundational ideas closer to real systems and real needs.
