01 A CENTER FOR TRUSTWORTHY AUTONOMY

Intelligence for
the real world.

The Center for Robotic Intelligent Systems with Trustworthy Autonomy (RISTRA) connects researchers across The University of New Mexico to advance robotics and intelligent autonomous systems.

Our work brings together robotics, AI, control, and human-centered research to help autonomous systems sense, decide, and act in complex environments.
