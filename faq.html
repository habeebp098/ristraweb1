What does RISTRA stand for?

Center for Robotic Intelligent Systems with Trustworthy Autonomy.

What areas does RISTRA bring together?

Robotics, intelligent systems, autonomous decision-making, and research on making autonomy trustworthy.

What does “trustworthy autonomy” mean?

It describes autonomous systems designed to be dependable, safe, understandable, and responsive to people and their operating environments.

Who is part of the RISTRA community?

Faculty, staff, students, and collaborators from across disciplines. Visit the Faculty and Staff, Students, and Collaborators pages.
