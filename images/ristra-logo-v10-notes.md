# RISTRA logo concept 10

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent PNG deliverables; these are raster assets, not SVG vectors.

## Visual rationale

- Robot sensor head: robotic intelligence across different physical platforms.
- Circular feedback arrows: the continuing sense–decide–act cycle of autonomy.
- Integrated check: verification and trustworthy operation.
- Crimson RISTRA acronym: alignment with the site's UNM palette; no expanded title in the logo.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a finished, professionally art-directed university research center logo for RISTRA (Center for Robotic Intelligent Systems with Trustworthy Autonomy at University of New Mexico). This is a fresh brand concept, not a scene or mockup.

Make ONE horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, wide landscape, with artwork tightly framed and only modest clear space. Emblem at left, exact word "RISTRA" at right, no other visible text.

Meaningful emblem: a serious, immediately recognizable geometric robot sensor head with a dark horizontal visor and two clear optical sensors. It represents robotic intelligence broadly, not any particular vehicle or industrial arm. Integrate the head inside ONE strong flowing circular autonomy loop. The loop suggests perception, decision, and action; its terminal should resolve elegantly into a check/verification shape that is part of the same mark. Design the head, loop, and verification as ONE coherent graphic silhouette, with intelligent negative space and balanced weights. It must look like a distinctive professional research identity, not assembled stock icons. Make the robot mature and technical rather than cute. No smile, no toy or mascot.

Wordmark: "RISTRA" spelled R I S T R A, all six letters in UNM crimson #BA0C2F. Confident custom geometric/slab-inspired uppercase typography, compact spacing, excellent readability. No full center name, no tagline, no other words.

Palette: UNM crimson #BA0C2F, charcoal #29272B, and transparent negative space only. Flat vector-style edges, solid fills, clean shapes, no shading, gradients, shadows, 3D, metallic texture, photographs or background plate. Do not add gold, mountains, chile peppers, wheel tracks, arms, tiny vehicles, generic network diagrams or many small nodes. Keep the icon bold enough to read at a 50-pixel height, with few meaningful details. The emblem should be about the same height as the wordmark or slightly taller. Prioritize excellent typographic composition and professional logo design.

## Final logo refinement prompt

Refine this logo, preserving its core idea: robot sensor head within an autonomy loop with an integrated check, plus the exact acronym RISTRA in UNM crimson #BA0C2F. Keep a genuinely transparent background.

Change styling and proportions for a respected UNIVERSITY RESEARCH CENTER: a calmer sophisticated academic identity, flat vector-like solid fills. Replace the large complex helmet with a simplified geometric robot sensor head: clean outline, dark visor, two optical sensors, a few purposeful features, no chin armor or tactical helmet segmentation. Keep the circular feedback loop and the obvious check integrated into that loop, using crimson and charcoal only. This must remain clearly a robot, but universal enough to represent many robotic and autonomous systems.

Crucial layout correction: SHRINK THE ENTIRE EMBLEM so it is only about 1.15 times the CAPITAL LETTER HEIGHT of the wordmark, not 2.5 times. Enlarge the wordmark relative to the emblem. Use elegant robust uppercase slab-serif or clean academic typography with careful kerning, no futuristic stencil cuts, no italic/gaming typography. All letters RISTRA must be crimson. No expanded name, no tagline, no further text.

A very clean horizontal logo, emblem left and wordmark right, centered on the same optical axis, modest gap, tight transparent crop with minimal empty border. No gradients, no shine, no mockup, no drop shadows, no background panel.
